Primary Objective of the Questionnaire

	To quantitatively assess the needs, preferences, and concerns of 
	IUT students, Cafeteria staff, admin, and 
	inventory managers regarding the current operations and 
	potential digital transformation of the Cafeteria system.

	This helped validate insights gathered through interviews and 
	observations, and to identify common pain points and system requirements 
	across all roles like : food quality, queue management, 
	inventory tracking, and feedback collection.

	Would you like to order meal online through a student portal or app?

	What features would you find most helpful in a digital Cafeteria system?



🍽️ Key Outcomes From Student Interviews
✅ Demand for better management 
Students emphasized the need for a system working on food and service

😟 Concern
Frequent complaints about poor food quality , hygiene and availability.

⭐ Top Feature Requests
View menu and food/service rating system.
Digital queue/slot booking to avoid long lines.


👨‍🍳 Key Outcomes From  Cafeteria Staff Interviews

✅ Need for Balanced Counter Load & Predictable Meal Count
Staff highlighted frequent overcrowding at one counter and the challenge of estimating daily meal turnout.

😟 Concern
Current system lacks any  data on student meal plans 
(who is expected to show up),
causing both under-preparation and over-preparation of food.

⭐ Top Feature Requests

Queue balancing system.
Dashboard showing meal orders and counts 
Trackable complaint history.


👨‍🍳 Key Outcomes From  inventory manager  Interviews
✅ Need for Real-Time Stock Tracking
Managers struggle with forecasting due to lack of visibility on current stock levels and consumption rates.

😟 Concern
Manual inventory updates often cause overstocking or sudden shortages; no alerts for low or expiring items.

⭐ Top Feature Requests

Real-time stock dashboard.
Auto-alerts for low stock or expiry.
Usage-based forecasting tools.




🗂️ Key Outcomes From Admin Interviews
✅ Need for Operational Oversight & Budget Control
Admins want tools to monitor overall service quality,  track budget and costs.

😟 Concern
Lack of a centralized system makes it hard to detect inventory discrepancies, validate budget use, or monitor feedback .

⭐ Top Feature Requests

Budget and inventory audit report
Feedback report
